# Architecture decision record

- Title, date, owner and status:
- Context, user outcome and constraints:
- Relevant invariant and quality attribute:
- Options considered (at least two credible options):
- Selected option and why it satisfies the constraint:
- Benefits and costs: operational burden, latency, consistency, security, resources:
- Boundaries, ownership and dependencies:
- Verification/fitness check:
- Revisit trigger measured in load, failures, cost or team needs:
- Compatibility/migration/rollback implications:
- Sources and which parts are local judgment:

“Industry best practice” without context and evidence is not a decision rationale.
